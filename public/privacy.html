Privacy Policy

Transparency regarding user privacy, Google OAuth 2.0 data handling, and cloud library synchronization on MusiSurf.

Effective Date: November 21, 2026 • Application: https://musi-surf.vercel.app • Version 1.1

01. Introduction & Platform Overview

Welcome to MusiSurf, accessible online at https://musi-surf.vercel.app. MusiSurf is a modern web music streaming and playlist platform designed for high-fidelity audio playback, synchronized lyrics discovery, and cross-device music library persistence.

We deeply respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy discloses our data practices, specifically how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your data when you visit our website, sign in via Google OAuth or email, create playlists, and save liked songs.

Core Commitment: MusiSurf does not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information or Google user data. We collect only the minimum data necessary to authenticate your account and sync your personal music library.

02. Google User Data Accessed

MusiSurf allows users to optionally authenticate using Google OAuth 2.0 (powered by Neon Built-In Auth). When you choose to sign in with Google, MusiSurf requests access strictly to basic, non-sensitive profile scopes (openid, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.profile, and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email):

Data Field Source Purpose & Usage
Email Address Google Account Profile Serves as your primary unique account identifier to log you in and manage library sync.
Full / Display Name Google Account Profile Used to personalize your interface and display your name on user-created playlists.
Profile Picture (Avatar URL) Google Account Profile Displayed in the top navigation avatar and profile settings dropdown.
Google Account ID (Subject) Google Identity Token Secure internal identifier mapping your authenticated sessions to your playlists.
Strictly No Sensitive Scopes: MusiSurf never requests, reads, accesses, or modifies sensitive or restricted Google user data. We do NOT access your Gmail messages, Google Drive documents, Google Calendar, Contacts, Google Photos, or location records.

03. How We Use Your Data

Your Google account information and personal library records are processed solely for legitimate application functionality:

  • User Authentication: Verifying your identity, issuing expiring secure session cookies, and maintaining your signed-in state across browser reloads.
  • Profile Personalization: Displaying your avatar, chosen name, and email within the user dashboard.
  • Library Persistence & Multi-Device Sync: Saving the songs you mark as liked ("Liked Songs") and custom playlists you create (titles, descriptions, track orders) so you can access them from any browser or device.
  • Security & Abuse Prevention: Enforcing rate limits, preventing automated bot abuse, and protecting our streaming infrastructure against denial-of-service attempts.

MusiSurf does not use your data for commercial profiling, targeted marketing, advertising tracking, or data-broker distribution.

04. Data Storage & Security

We treat the security of your account and personal library with utmost diligence:

  • Encrypted Database: User accounts, session records, playlists, and liked tracks are stored in a secured Neon PostgreSQL cloud database. All connections to the database use mandatory TLS 1.3/SSL encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest.
  • Session Integrity: Authentication tokens are signed, time-limited, and transmitted exclusively via secure HTTP-only cookies (SameSite=Lax), guarding against cross-site scripting (XSS) and token exfiltration.
  • Local Browser Storage: Cached audio files for offline listening, volume sliders, and equalizer preferences are stored locally in your browser's IndexedDB and LocalStorage, remaining entirely under your control on your device.

05. Data Sharing & Limited Use Disclosure

MusiSurf has a strict policy regarding third-party data sharing:

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or Google user data to any third parties under any circumstances.
  • We do not share Google user data with third-party artificial intelligence (AI) models, large language models (LLMs), or machine learning systems for model training.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to external services, except as strictly necessary to fulfill the application's core database storage (Neon PostgreSQL) and cloud hosting (Vercel serverless platform).
“MusiSurf's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.”

You can review the official policy details at the Google API Services User Data Policy.

06. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

We believe users should possess total sovereignty over their personal library and identity data:

  • Instant In-App Playlist Deletion: You can delete any playlist at any time by opening the playlist options menu and selecting “Delete Playlist”. This permanently purges the playlist and its track relations from our cloud database immediately.
  • Instant In-App Liked Tracks Removal: Clicking the heart icon on any liked song instantly unlikes the track and purges the row from your saved account library.
  • Complete Account Deletion: You have the right to request full erasure of your account, email profile, and all associated library history. You can submit an account deletion request at any time by contacting our support team at support@musi-surf.vercel.app or hritikraj656@gmail.com with the subject line “Account Deletion Request”. All database rows linked to your account ID will be permanently purged within 24 hours of verification.
