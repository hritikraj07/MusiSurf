Official Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 21, 2026 • Version 1.0

01. Introduction

Welcome to MusiSurf (accessible at https://musi-surf.vercel.app). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it is stored securely, and the choices you have concerning your personal data when using MusiSurf web and mobile applications.

MusiSurf does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for advertising or commercial monetization purposes.

02. Information We Collect

We only collect the minimal information necessary to deliver high-fidelity audio playback, user authentication, and persistent personal music libraries:

  • Account Information (Google OAuth): When you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your email address, display name, and avatar URL as provided by your Google profile. We do not access your contacts, files, or Google Drive.
  • Account Information (Email / Password): When you create an account directly with email, we store your email address, chosen display name, and a securely salted and hashed password representation.
  • User Library Data: Songs you like ("Liked Songs"), custom playlists you create (titles, descriptions, track listings), and playback preferences.
  • Local & Offline Data: Tracks saved for offline listening, volume preferences, and audio equalization configurations stored strictly within your browser's IndexedDB and LocalStorage.
  • Technical Information: Standard HTTP request logs (IP address, user-agent string, device operating system) recorded strictly for network security, rate limiting, and debugging service reliability.

03. How We Use Your Information

We use the data we collect solely for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate your identity and maintain secure sessions across your devices.
  • To save, synchronize, and restore your Liked Tracks and custom Playlists using cloud database storage.
  • To deliver edge-free, seamless audio playback and synchronized lyrics.
  • To protect against abuse, denial-of-service attempts, and unauthorized API exploitation.

04. Third-Party Services & Storage

MusiSurf utilizes reputable infrastructure providers to operate:

  • Google Identity Services: Provides secure single sign-on (OAuth 2.0).
  • Neon Database (PostgreSQL): Stores verified user accounts, session tokens, playlists, and liked tracks in an encrypted cloud database environment with SSL encryption in transit and at rest.
  • Media Providers & Content Delivery: Audio streams and song metadata are retrieved from publicly accessible public music repositories and content delivery networks.

05. Cookies & Session Management

MusiSurf uses standard HTTP-only session cookies (such as better-auth.session_token) to verify authentication state. These cookies are strictly functional and security-oriented; we do not employ third-party tracking or advertising pixels.

06. Data Retention & Your Rights

You have full control over your data stored in MusiSurf:

  • Playlists: You can delete any playlist at any time, which permanently removes the playlist and its track relations from our database.
  • Liked Tracks: You can unlike any track at any time to instantly remove it from your saved account records.
  • Account Deletion: You can request complete deletion of your account and associated database records by contacting us at the address below.

07. Contact Us
